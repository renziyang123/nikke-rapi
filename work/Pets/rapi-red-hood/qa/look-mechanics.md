# Rapi gaze mechanics

Boot soles, legs, skirt belt and torso remain frontal and planted at the idle registration. Keep the attached compact weapon at the same shoulder and all hair connected. The separate head yaws at the neck and pitches toward the target, with natural matching eye surfaces, eyebrows, nose tip, chin, bangs and beret following. No whole-body turntable, no replacement eyes or pupil-only gaze.

Up: face lifted, chin raised, lower edge of chin visible, irises toward upper lids. Down: chin lowered toward tie, hat top more visible, eyes and nose directed down. Right/left: head turns toward viewer's right/left with nose visibly offset that way; opposite cheek naturally occluded, torso remains front. Diagonals combine pitch and yaw with both axes present. Hair follows subtly; maintain compact width and stable scale and feet baseline.
